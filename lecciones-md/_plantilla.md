## Vocabulario
weather = clima
rain = lluvia
sunny = soleado

## Listen and Repeat
It's raining today. = Hoy está lloviendo.
The weather is nice. = El clima está lindo.

## Listen and Type
It's very cold outside. = Hace mucho frío afuera.

## Listen and Translate
I love sunny days. = Me encantan los días soleados.
